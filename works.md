# Works

Each entry states the public credit as it exists today.

## For All Mankind (2023–2024)

Rishabh Chakrabarty is the named artist of *For All Mankind*, a large-scale immersive installation using a stereo-3D generative model trained on Martian terrain imagery, shown with holographic glasses and spatial sound.

- Bright Festival, Florence, 2024: https://www.brightfestival.com/art_event/for-all-mankind-rishabh-chakrabarty/
- NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI, author of record: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/105442
- Sound design on the Bright Festival page: William Russell

## EnPerspectiva (2024)

Large-scale light and spatial-sound installation at Bright Festival CONNECT / Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig. Public credit line: “Rish x LedPulse x Omni Soundlab x Symbiotism.”

https://connect.brightfestival.com/art/enperspectiva/
