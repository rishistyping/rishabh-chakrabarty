# Rishabh Chakrabarty

Rishabh Chakrabarty is a Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. He is the artist of *For All Mankind* (Bright Festival, Florence, 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI). He appears in Refik Anadol Studio production credits for *Unsupervised* at the Museum of Modern Art and *Sense of Space* at the Venice Architecture Biennale, and is a UniverseTBD collaborator on pathfinder and AstroLLaVA.

He also goes by Rish. Public handle: [@rishistyping](https://github.com/rishistyping).

## Current

As of August 2026, he is based in Paris and working across computational media art and AI-for-science collaboration. Dated public records place him as a UniverseTBD collaborator and as the named artist of *For All Mankind*.
